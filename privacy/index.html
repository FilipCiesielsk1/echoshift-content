EchoShift — Privacy Policy

Developer: FiiFi Games
Last updated: October 21, 2026
Privacy contact: fiifigamesprivacy@gmail.com

This policy explains how the current version of EchoShift uses information when you play, download SIGNALS puzzles, or contact us.

Data Controller

EchoShift is developed and published by FiiFi Games, an independent game development brand operated by Filip Ciesielski, an individual based in Poland. FiiFi Games is a publishing brand, not a separate company. This individual is the data controller for the processing described as our responsibility in this policy.

For privacy-related inquiries, contact fiifigamesprivacy@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

EchoShift saves information locally to remember your progress and preferences:

  • Campaign progress, completed levels, best times, stars, Echo counts and tutorial status.
  • Game statistics, including play time, completions, resets, restarts and laser deaths. Local run records can also include unfinished attempts.
  • Achievements, earned and spent virtual stars, cosmetic items and equipped appearances.
  • SIGNALS completion records, scores, content versions and downloaded puzzles for offline play.
  • Music, sound and vibration settings.
  • Random save identifiers, revision numbers and timestamps used to maintain save consistency. These are generated by the game, not read from your device's hardware identifier.

The current version does not automatically upload these gameplay saves, statistics or settings. Player and Echo movement recordings are used during gameplay; they are not sent to a server.

Accounts and cloud saves

You do not create a FiiFi Games account to play EchoShift. Google Play Games sign-in and Google cloud saves are disabled in this version. The locally stored game save is not an online account. If these services are enabled in a future version, we will update this policy to describe the resulting data processing.

Content downloads and network information

When you open SIGNALS while online, EchoShift automatically checks its public content catalog and downloads available puzzles from GitHub Pages. Downloaded content can be played offline. Requests identify the catalog or puzzle file being requested; the game does not attach your local save identifier, gameplay statistics or Google identity to these requests.

Internet connections expose information such as your IP address and the requested URL to the hosting service. GitHub states that GitHub Pages visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Opening this policy in your browser also connects to GitHub Pages and is subject to your browser's behavior.

See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub General Privacy Statement for GitHub's practices, retention and privacy choices. FiiFi Games does not operate a separate gameplay-save server for this version.

Other services and permissions

This version does not implement advertising, behavioral analytics, or automatic crash-report uploads. It does not request your contacts, camera, microphone or device location. Internet access supports content downloads; vibration supports optional haptic feedback.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Our contact mailbox uses Gmail, operated by Google. We use correspondence to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. Google's handling of its services is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Development builds may offer manual diagnostic or playtest exports. These are not automatic uploads: information leaves the device only if you choose to share an export, for example through your email app.

Legal bases under the GDPR

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, the legal basis depends on the purpose:

  • Support correspondence: processing your email address, message and any attachments needed to answer ordinary questions relies on our legitimate interest in providing support and resolving reported issues (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Privacy requests: processing necessary to respond to GDPR rights requests and demonstrate compliance relies on our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Content delivery: initiating the network requests needed to deliver SIGNALS puzzles and this policy relies on our legitimate interest in providing reliable, secure access to game content and information (Article 6(1)(f)). GitHub's processing for its own purposes is governed separately by its privacy statement.
  • Local game information: to the extent this information is personal data for which we are responsible, processing needed to remember progress and preferences relies on our legitimate interest in providing game continuity and the features you use (Article 6(1)(f)). Local storage does not give us remote access to your saves.

Legitimate interests apply only where the processing is necessary and your interests, rights and freedoms do not override them. You may object as explained below. Sending an email or using the game is not treated as blanket consent to unrelated processing.

Retention and deletion

Local game data

Local progress, settings and downloaded content remain in the app's storage until removed or replaced. Local run records have no automatic expiry. To remove the app's local data on Android, use the device's app settings to clear EchoShift's storage. This removes local progress and downloaded puzzles. Uninstalling normally removes app-local storage; independently created copies or device transfers must be managed separately.

NEW GAME restarts Campaign progress; it is not a complete data-deletion function. It preserves account-lifetime items such as achievements and virtual currency, and does not erase SIGNALS progress or all local statistics.

Support and privacy correspondence

We retain privacy-related and support correspondence, including your email address, messages and attachments, for as long as necessary to handle your request. We delete routine correspondence and attachments no later than 12 months after the matter is resolved. We may retain specific information for longer where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, with an appropriate legal basis and subject to applicable legal requirements.

We review the mailbox at least once every quarter and manually delete correspondence in Gmail according to this policy. There is no automatic system that deletes messages on our behalf according to the 12-month deadline. The deadline applies to deletion from the mailbox, including its Trash; Google's handling of any remaining provider-managed copies is subject to its own policies.

Clearing app storage does not delete correspondence you have sent us. You may contact us to request erasure where the law provides that right; the 12-month maximum does not delay a valid request for earlier erasure.

Third-party data

GitHub determines retention of logs it holds for its own purposes under its policies. FiiFi Games does not have direct access to all GitHub Pages visitor logs and cannot erase records outside its control. Google handles data within Gmail according to its applicable service terms and privacy policy; our responsibility for correspondence we hold is described above.

GitHub and Google may process information outside Poland and the European Economic Area, as described in their linked privacy policies. Clearing app storage does not delete their records. Requests concerning their own processing can be directed to those providers.

There is no in-game server-account deletion function because this version does not create a FiiFi Games account.

Security

Remote content is requested over HTTPS and validated before use. Local saves use the application's storage area. These measures do not guarantee absolute security. Local save files are not encrypted by the game, so protect your device and any copies you make.

Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies, you can request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure where applicable, and restriction of processing. You can object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests. Portability applies where its legal conditions are met. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions set out in the GDPR.

To exercise your rights concerning data for which we are responsible, email fiifigamesprivacy@gmail.com. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. We cannot remotely access or delete saves stored only on your device; use your device's app-storage settings for those files. NEW GAME is not a complete data-deletion action.

You may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, including in the country of your habitual residence, place of work or alleged infringement. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO). See UODO's complaint guidance. Requests concerning a provider's own records may need to be directed to that provider.

Children's privacy

EchoShift does not ask players to provide their name, age or contact details to play. The content-download practices described above also apply when a child uses the game. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to FiiFi Games can contact us to discuss its removal. This statement does not establish an age rating or target audience.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when relevant features or data practices change and revise the date above. For privacy questions, contact FiiFi Games at fiifigamesprivacy@gmail.com.
